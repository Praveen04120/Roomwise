Viva & Architecture Overview

How Roomwise Determines Classroom Availability

In a large college building with 9 floors and over 140 classrooms, students and faculty often waste valuable break periods physically searching for empty rooms for study circles, placement preparation, or makeup classes.

Core C++ Scheduling Engine: Roomwise runs a deterministic interval intersection algorithm in C++. Operating hours (08:00 to 18:00) are converted to 24-hour minutes after midnight. A classroom is verified as Occupied if and only if a scheduled class overlaps the requested interval (start_A < end_B && end_A > start_B). Adjacent classes (e.g. 10:00 to 11:00 and 11:00 to 12:00) produce no conflict. Classrooms lacking verified schedule coverage are explicitly classified as Unavailable rather than falsely reported empty.
